# Siarhei Besarab | Media Archive

**Independent Scientist · Science Communicator · Open-Source Developer**

This repository hosts the source code for the personal website and media archive of **Siarhei Besarab** ([@steanlab](https://github.com/steanlab)).

🌐 **Website:** [steanlab.github.io](https://steanlab.github.io)

## About

Siarhei Besarab is an independent scientist, science communicator, and open-source developer working at the intersection of chemistry, materials science, technology, and critical risk analysis.

This website brings together his professional work and public contributions, providing a central reference for journalists, researchers, collaborators, and readers.

## What's Included

- **Interviews & Media Appearances** — interviews, expert commentary, and contributions to news coverage.
- **Publications** — articles, essays, and other published work.
- **Science & Technology** — commentary on scientific research, emerging technologies, and engineering.
- **Nuclear Safety** — expert contributions on nuclear energy, radiation risks, and nuclear safety.
- **Projects & Research** — information about scientific work and open-source projects.

## Areas of Expertise

- Chemistry and materials science
- Surface and colloid chemistry
- Porous materials and adsorption processes
- Nuclear and radiation safety
- Critical risks and emerging technologies
- Science communication and open-source development
