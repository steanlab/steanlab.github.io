## How to credit

- English: **Siarhei Besarab**
- Russian: **Сергей Бесараб**
- Belarusian: **Сяргей Бесараб**
- The surname is written with a single «s»: Besarab, not Bessarab (Бесараб, not Бессараб).
- Suggested caption: *Siarhei Besarab, independent scientist and research chemist (colloid and surface chemistry), or radiation chemist*.

## Biography

**Short.** Siarhei Besarab is an independent scientist and research chemist in colloid and surface chemistry, author of 12 patents. He is a regular expert for Belsat, RFE/RL and Deutsche Welle on nuclear safety, global catastrophic risks and technology analysis. Based in the European Union.

**Medium.** Siarhei Besarab is an independent scientist and research chemist in colloid and surface chemistry. He holds master's degrees in environmental chemistry and in pharmaceutical chemistry and worked as a researcher at the Institute of General and Inorganic Chemistry of the National Academy of Sciences of Belarus in 2010–2020. He is the author of a scientific monograph, more than 50 scientific and over 200 popular-science publications, and a co-author of 12 patents. He is a regular expert for Belsat, RFE/RL, Deutsche Welle, Voice of America, Zerkalo.io and other outlets on nuclear and radiation safety, the environment, climate and biosafety, hosts the rubric «Топология сингулярности» on Belsat, founded the science-communication project LAB66 and spoke at TEDxUlicaMińska in 2026. He lives in the European Union.
